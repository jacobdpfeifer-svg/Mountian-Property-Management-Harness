# For the owner

The operations layer is a useful read-only safety net, but it is not ready to be
trusted as a production compliance or turnover system without operator confirmation.
The pricing boundary is conservative: operations can hold, demote, or block a night,
and it does not close inventory or send guest messages.

Before relying on it, confirm the clean times, crew size, travel, hot tubs, rates,
holiday coverage, and every profile marked `basis: estimate`. Confirm the jurisdiction
for 312 Northwoods and 300 Northwoods. Cloud 9 is configured as Fraser, but that also
needs operator confirmation.

The three compliance packs are expressly unreviewed transcriptions. They must be checked
by a responsible person, and legal questions should go to operator / legal review. The
Fraser pack is missing several safety items shown on the town page, including smoke and
CO detectors, extinguishers, and egress requirements. The checker reports evidence and
deadlines; it does not establish legal compliance.

Guesty Tasks, Breezeway, and Turno adapters are read-only and fail closed on missing
credentials or unexpected payloads. They were built from public documentation, not a
live tenant. Verify a recorded export or live account before using their fields for
decisions.

The strongest immediate fixes in this audit are DST-aware local turn windows and locked
portfolio scoping. The next operational work is live-fixture integration testing,
stale-feed health gating, and a reviewed compliance register.
