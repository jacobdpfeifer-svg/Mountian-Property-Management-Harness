# Buyer workflows

Status: desk research only; no vendor account or buyer interview was used in this audit.

The repo’s 2026-09-30 operator sample emphasizes reliable turns, human escalation,
owner trust, and permits. The product should differentiate on the connection between
operational evidence and conservative pricing, while integrating rather than replacing
task systems.

Sources opened or referenced for follow-up: [Breezeway developer portal](https://developer.breezeway.io/),
[Turno API docs](https://apidocs.turnoverbnb.com/), [Guesty Open API](https://open-api.guesty.com/),
[Operto](https://operto.com/), [Hostaway](https://www.hostaway.com/),
[Granicus Host Compliance](https://granicus.com/product/host-compliance/),
[Deckard](https://deckard.com/), and [Avalara MyLodgeTax](https://www.avalara.com/us/en/products/my-lodge-tax.html).

Recommendation: integrate task execution and permit evidence where available; keep the
pricing/restriction bridge as the differentiator, subject to measured false-restriction
cost and human override.
