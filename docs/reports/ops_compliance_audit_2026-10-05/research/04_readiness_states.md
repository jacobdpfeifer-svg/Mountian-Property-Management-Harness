# Readiness states

Verdict: keep the seven-state history; simplify the operator UI. The stored distinction
between risk, inspection, out-of-service, remediation, and verified recovery is valuable
for auditability. A 7 a.m. phone flow should ask for one of: risk observed, inspection
needed, out of service, remediation started, or verify ready—with the system retaining
the detailed transition and evidence row.

The current named-actor and sensor-verification rules are a good boundary. Add a compact
current-state card, evidence photo/task links, and stale-sensor warnings.
