# Colorado mountain compliance scope

Status: source review, not legal advice. Every pack remains unverified until an operator
or qualified reviewer accepts it.

The [official Winter Park STR page](https://wpgov.com/good-neighbor/) says registration
is required before advertising/renting, the registration number must appear in
advertising, and registrations expire September 30. The [official Winter Park business
page](https://wpgov.com/doing-business/rent-your-home/) says a business license and
sales-tax remittance apply within town limits.

The [official Fraser STR page](https://www.frasercolorado.com/303/Short-Term-Rental)
lists owner/manager contact availability, parking, inspection, posted permit number,
smoke detectors, CO detectors, extinguishers, operable egress, wildlife-proof refuse,
business or STR operating license, state sales-tax license, and penalties. The current
Fraser pack lacks explicit smoke/CO/extinguisher/egress requirements and should be
expanded after review.

The [official Grand County STR page](https://www.co.grand.co.us/943/Short-Term-Rentals)
was opened, but the page content was not sufficiently extractable in this run to verify
each requirement. Grand County entries therefore remain unverified.

Permit caps, occupancy, parking, septic, tax, insurance, and safety questions that turn
on legal interpretation are **operator / legal review** items, not automated verdicts.
