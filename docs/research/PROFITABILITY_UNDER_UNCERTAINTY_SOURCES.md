# Profitability-under-uncertainty source register

This research constrains ranges in the synthetic model; it does not replace settled portfolio data. Every output is `ASSUMPTION-BASED`.

| Topic | Public evidence | Permitted use | Not permitted |
|---|---|---|---|
| Winter Park market | AirDNA reports 43% trailing-12-month occupancy, $379 ADR, $163 RevPAR and 1,632 active listings (updated 2026-10-13). | Broad market anchor and downside context. | Forecast a 5-6 bedroom luxury home or call it a comp set. |
| State tourism | Colorado Tourism reports 96.8m 2025 visitors, up 1.4%. | Directional macro scenario context. | Convert statewide visits to bookings. |
| Airport demand | DEN reports 82.43m passengers in 2025. | Weak arrival-demand stress context. | Treat passengers as Winter Park guests. |
| Snowpack | NRCS publishes daily Berthoud Summit SNOTEL snow depth and SWE. | Define snow stress worlds and observed-time features. | Convert snow depth directly into ADR or profit. |
| Road access | CDOT publishes real-time I-70 Mountain Corridor conditions. | Define disruption/closure stress worlds. | Fit a booking-loss coefficient without property outcomes. |
| Airbnb fee | Airbnb says most single-fee hosts pay 15.5%. | Center a broad OTA fee range. | Assert the portfolio paid that rate. |
| Management fee | SkyRun Denver publishes 20% gross reservation rent and separates physical costs. | Support a wide 15-25% planning range. | Infer this portfolio's contract or bundled costs. |
| Regulation | Fraser publishes $350/bedroom STR registration after Oct. 2025. | Contingency/cost review. | Allocate it before jurisdiction is confirmed. |

The model intentionally leaves cleaning, maintenance, turns, sellable nights, system cost, elasticity and pricing lift as ranges. The public sources do not identify them. The smallest useful real-data replacement is one de-identified, fully settled month per home: booked/available nights, payout deductions, owner-statement net, cleaning/turn invoices, and annual system cost.
