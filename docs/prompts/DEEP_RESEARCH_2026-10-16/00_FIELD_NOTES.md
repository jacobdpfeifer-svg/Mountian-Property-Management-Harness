# Field notes: operator cold calls, 2026-10-16

Source: the operator's calls to Winter Park property-management companies. The richest call was with
a local Winter Park vacation-rental management company (unnamed here because this repo is public).
Everything below is **one practitioner's claim**. Treat each claim as a hypothesis to test, not a
fact.

| # | Claim | How to test it (owner) |
|---|---|---|
| H1 | Winter Park is a **middle-class** market compared with Vail, Aspen or Breckenridge-luxury. | R7: rate distributions and ADR by market, guest income or origin data, Ikon vs Epic mix |
| H2 | Generic pricers (PriceLabs and the rest) **do not collect the data that moves this market** and weight the wrong factors. PMs are not getting the best achievable rate. | R4 teardown + R6 ranking |
| H3 | There are **too many possible factors**. The real problem is picking the right ones. | R6 (value-of-information ranking) |
| H4 | **Gas prices** are suppressing Front Range drive-up demand. It is a drive-to resort. | R7: I-70 / US-40 Berthoud Pass traffic vs Colorado retail gasoline, controlling for snow and holidays |
| H5 | About **70% of annual revenue** is earned in the winter months. | R7: Grand County / Winter Park lodging-tax monthly series, DestiMetrics-type summaries, our own per-home aggregates |
| H6 | Airlines and hotels have used **sophisticated RM** far longer than STR. STR pricing is about 10 years old and immature. | R1–R4 |
| H7 | A **hyper-specialized** engine for one market can beat generic pricers. | S1: say where specialization actually yields an edge and where it does not |

Operator direction from the same day:
- Research airline, hotel and other industries' price optimization comprehensively, then compare
  it with what we have.
- Build out the hyper-specialized edge. Consider every data stream that could productively inform
  price, **geopolitical streams included**.
- Find the **highest-value** data streams, then use them to their maximum potential.
- No light research. Go as deep as possible. The goal is the most profitable engine possible.
