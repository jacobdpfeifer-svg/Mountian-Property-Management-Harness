# V: Adversarial review of a dossier

Output: `docs/research/deep_2026-10/reviews/<dossier-id>_review.md`

You are a skeptical reviewer. Your job is to stop shallow or wrong research from reaching the
build plan.

1. **Depth audit.** Check every MUST in `00_DEPTH_STANDARD.md` yourself. Do not trust the
   author's self-audit. Count sources by class.
2. **Citation spot-check.** Open at least **15** cited URLs, chosen at random plus every
   load-bearing claim. Record for each: supports the claim / partly / does not / dead or
   fabricated.
3. **Coverage gaps.** Compare the dossier with its prompt's "Cover at minimum" list and name
   every item that is missing or thin. Do your own searches (at least 10) for important material
   the author missed, and list what you found with citations.
4. **Reasoning.** Look for overclaiming, vendor-reported lift taken at face value, airline or hotel
   methods declared transferable without addressing thin data or a 3-unit inventory, and
   recommendations with no measurement plan.
5. **Verdict.** ACCEPT / ACCEPT WITH FIXES / REJECT. Give a numbered fix list the author can act
   on directly.

Be specific and cite. Do not rewrite the dossier yourself.
